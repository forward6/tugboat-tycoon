Privacy

What Tugboat Tycoon keeps, in plain words.

Playing

You can play without signing up or telling us anything about yourself. Your games are saved in your browser on your own device.

The scoreboard

Nothing is sent until you make your first move in the game (a job for a boat, a purchase, a loan). From then on, so that you appear on the public scoreboard, the game sends, every few minutes while you play:

  • a random player number the game makes up, and the name you play under (a settler's name like Ewan Kerr unless you change it; names that are rude are refused);
  • your company's name, the game year and month, what your company is worth, your boats, and how long you have played;
  • the kind of device (iPad, iPhone, Mac and so on);
  • roughly where you play from: the province or state and country of your internet connection. Never your city, street or internet address. You can hide even that, from the scoreboard.

Online saves

Your last few games are also saved on our server, so you can bring one back if your device loses it, or play it on another device. Only your own devices can read them.

Signing in

Signing in is optional, and uses a passkey: your device's Face ID, Touch ID or lock. We never see your face, fingerprint or code; your device only proves it is the same one. We keep no email address and no password. If you set up a passkey we give you a recovery code; we keep only a scrambled form of it, and cannot tell you what it was.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no tracking across other sites, no analytics companies.
  • We don't sell or share anything with anyone.

Taking yourself off

You can take yourself off the scoreboard from the scoreboard in the game. To have everything about you deleted, ask the person who runs the game, and give your player name.
